ABOUT LISA

Twenty years in the NHS taught me where change actually starts.

Nursing taught me that health is never just clinical — it's shaped by everything happening outside the treatment room: sleep, work, pressure, confidence, and the daily patterns nobody has time to examine.

Time and again, I've watched people struggle not from a lack of willpower, but because no one gave them the time or space to understand what was really in their way. That's rarely more information. It's honest attention, and someone who isn't trying to sell them anything.

You don't need to have it figured out before we start. That's the point of the call.
