Coaching, simply put.

CHOOSING A COACH

Coaching isn't a regulated profession. Anyone can call themselves a coach, and there's no register you have to be on. There are excellent coaches without formal training — but it's worth knowing what you're choosing between.

A few things worth asking any coach you're considering:

01

What training have they done, and who accredited it?

02

Are they a member of a professional body with a code of ethics — and somewhere you could complain if you needed to?

03

Are they insured?

04

Are they clear about what coaching isn't, and when they'd refer you elsewhere?
