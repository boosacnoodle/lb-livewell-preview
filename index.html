About Lisa

Twenty years in healthcare taught me where change really begins.

I have spent more than two decades as a nurse in the NHS. I saw again and again how much of our health sits outside a clinic room — in sleep, work, pressure, confidence, and the small daily patterns that quietly shape everything else.

What people needed most was rarely more information. It was time, attention, and someone genuinely on their side.

You do not need to have everything worked out before beginning.
